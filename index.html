Castle & park

Książ Castle

One of the largest castles in Poland and the symbol of the region. A majestic residence surrounded by terraces, gardens and an extensive park, crossed by walking trails above the Pełcznica gorge.

The tour covers the grand halls and chambers, while seasonal exhibitions and the underground route draw visitors all year round. The ruins of the Old Książ castle lie right next door.

  • Must-see: the Maximilian Hall, the viewing terrace, the gardens and the Palm House.
  • Good to know: it is one of the largest structures of its kind in the country.
  • Best for: families, history and architecture enthusiasts.
Museum & technology

Old Mine

A science and art centre built on the site of the former "Julia" coal mine. It is the largest post-mining institution of its kind in Poland — combining the history of mining with a modern exhibition space.

Visitors learn about the history of coal extraction, descend into the underground corridors, and see historic mining machinery and equipment. The site also has a lively cultural calendar of concerts and exhibitions.

  • Must-see: the underground route, the winding tower and the machinery display.
  • Good to know: some routes require advance booking.
  • Best for: children, teenagers and technology fans.
Gardens

Wałbrzych Palm House

Set in the historic complex near Książ Castle — one of the most interesting collections of exotic plants in Lower Silesia. Palm trees, cacti, citrus plants and many other species grow inside the glass pavilions.

It is a great place for a walk outside the main season too — the warm interiors stay green even in winter, and the stables and park are just a short stroll away.

  • Must-see: the tall palms, the succulent collection and exotic flowers.
  • Good to know: easy to combine with a visit to the castle.
  • Best for: walkers and botany lovers.
Park & views

Jan III Sobieski Park

A popular spot for walks and relaxation in the very centre of Wałbrzych. From the park's paths you can admire the panorama of the city and the surrounding hills.

It is a starting point for many walks — trails leading toward the hills are close by, and the park itself is eagerly visited by locals and tourists alike in every season.

  • Must-see: the viewpoint over the city and the Wałbrzych Hills.
  • Good to know: a great place for a short break along the way.
  • Best for: families with children and anyone seeking relaxation.
Museum

Porcelain Museum

A museum presenting rich porcelain collections and the history of the Wałbrzych region, closely tied to the local ceramics industry.

The exhibitions show both historic vessels and figurines as well as the process of making porcelain. It is an interesting stop for anyone wanting to better understand the city's industrial identity.

  • Must-see: the decorated and everyday porcelain collections.
  • Good to know: the museum is housed in a historic palace.
  • Best for: lovers of craft and industrial history.
Peak & trails

Chełmiec Mountain

One of the characteristic peaks near Wałbrzych and a popular destination for hiking trips. At the summit you will find a viewing tower and a historic chapel.

The trails up Chełmiec lead through forests, and from the top there is a broad panorama over Wałbrzych and the surrounding mountain ranges. A good idea for an active day outdoors.

  • Must-see: the viewing tower and the panorama of the Wałbrzych Hills.
  • Good to know: bring comfortable shoes and water.
  • Best for: hikers and those who enjoy active leisure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much time do I need to tour Książ Castle?

Usually 2 to 3 hours — longer if you plan to stroll through the gardens, visit the Palm House, and walk toward the Old Książ ruins.

Is the Old Mine suitable for children?

Yes, the site is set up for family visits. Some underground routes are worth booking in advance — it is best to check the venue's current schedule online.

How do I get to Chełmiec?

The easiest way is by car to the trailhead, then on foot. The routes are marked, but sturdy footwear is recommended.

Are the attractions open all year round?

The castle, the Old Mine and the museums operate year-round, though opening hours change seasonally. The parks, the Palm House interior and the trails are accessible regardless of the weather, but it is worth checking any notices.

Summary

Wałbrzych and its surroundings offer a wide range of tourist attractions. Visitors can explore historic buildings, learn about the history of the region, admire nature, and spend their free time actively — in any season.
