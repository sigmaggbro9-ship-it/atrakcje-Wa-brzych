Must-see places

Places worth seeing

Six of the most frequently visited attractions in Wałbrzych and its immediate surroundings — from a grand castle to a mountain peak.

🏰 Castle & park

Książ Castle

One of the largest castles in Poland and the symbol of the region. A majestic residence surrounded by terraces, gardens and an extensive park, crossed by walking trails above the Pełcznica gorge. Seasonal exhibitions and the underground route draw visitors all year round.

  • Must-see: the Maximilian Hall and the gardens.
  • Good to know: the Old Książ ruins are next door.
  • Best for: families and architecture fans.
⛏️ Museum & technology

Old Mine

A science and art centre built on the site of the former "Julia" coal mine — the largest post-mining institution of its kind in Poland. Visitors descend into underground corridors and see historic machinery, while concerts and temporary exhibitions keep the site lively.

  • Must-see: the underground route and winding tower.
  • Good to know: some routes need advance booking.
  • Best for: teenagers and technology fans.
🌴 Gardens

Wałbrzych Palm House

Set in the historic complex near Książ Castle — one of the most interesting collections of exotic plants in Lower Silesia. Palm trees, cacti and citrus plants grow inside the glass pavilions, and the warm interiors stay green even in winter.

  • Must-see: the tall palms and succulents.
  • Good to know: easy to combine with the castle.
  • Best for: walkers and botany lovers.
🌳 Park & views

Jan III Sobieski Park

A popular spot for walks and relaxation in the very centre of Wałbrzych. From the park's paths you can admire the panorama of the city and the surrounding hills, and it is a natural starting point for longer walks.

  • Must-see: the viewpoint over the city.
  • Good to know: ideal for a short break.
  • Best for: families and quiet relaxation.
🏺 Museum

Porcelain Museum

A museum presenting rich porcelain collections and the history of the Wałbrzych region, tied to the local ceramics industry. The exhibitions show historic vessels and figurines as well as the process of making porcelain.

  • Must-see: the decorated porcelain collections.
  • Good to know: housed in a historic palace.
  • Best for: lovers of craft and history.
⛰️ Peak & trails

Chełmiec Mountain

One of the characteristic peaks near Wałbrzych and a popular destination for hiking trips. At the summit you will find a viewing tower and a historic chapel, and a broad panorama over the city and the surrounding ranges.

  • Must-see: the viewing tower and the panorama.
  • Good to know: bring solid shoes and water.
  • Best for: hikers and active leisure.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How much time do I need to tour Książ Castle?

Usually 2 to 3 hours — longer if you plan to stroll through the gardens, visit the Palm House, and walk toward the Old Książ ruins.

Is the Old Mine suitable for children?

Yes, the site is set up for family visits. Some underground routes are worth booking in advance — it is best to check the venue's current schedule online.

How do I get to Chełmiec?

The easiest way is by car to the trailhead, then on foot. The routes are marked, but sturdy footwear is recommended.

Are the attractions open all year round?

The castle, the Old Mine and the museums operate year-round, though opening hours change seasonally. The parks, the Palm House interior and the trails are accessible regardless of the weather, but it is worth checking any notices.

Do I need a car to see everything?

Not strictly — the castle, Palm House, museums and the centrally located park are reachable on foot or by public transport. For Chełmiec and the Old Mine, a car or a taxi is more convenient.
